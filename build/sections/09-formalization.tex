\section{Lean formalization}\label{app:formalization}

All theorems of the paper are formalized in Lean~4 (toolchain \texttt{v4.34.1}) in the
directory \path{lean/WangLogic/} of the repository given under Code availability.
\path{Basic.lean} covers Sections~\ref{sec:setting}--\ref{sec:foundation} except the extensions
below and uses only the core library. The other files use Mathlib~\cite{mathlib} (commit
\texttt{d13f23b7}): \path{Confirmation.lean} (\cref{rem:probability} and
\cref{sec:confirmation}), \path{Defeasible.lean} (\cref{sec:defeasible}), \path{Levels.lean}
(\cref{sec:levels}), \path{Quantum.lean}
(\cref{prop:qsound,prop:classical,prop:qhume}) and \path{QuantumExtensions.lean}
(\cref{prop:unitary,prop:nondistrib,prop:noise} and entropy invariance). No file contains
\texttt{sorry}, and every declaration depends at most on the axioms \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Quot.sound} and \texttt{Classical.choice}. The project builds with
\begin{center}\texttt{cd lean \&\& lake exe cache get \&\& lake build}\end{center}
The file \path{lean/ComparatorChallenges/InductionNotDeduction.json} states
\cref{thm:hume} for independent checking with the Comparator tool
(\url{https://github.com/leanprover/comparator}). Not formalized: Landauer's
principle, the inequality $H(T\mid S)\le H(T)$, and the physical implementations and experimental
proposals of \cref{sec:quantum}.

\paragraph{Conventions.} In \cref{sec:confirmation} the set of worlds is finite and a prior is a
strictly positive weight; probabilities are normalized sums of weights. In
\cref{sec:defeasible} a ranking takes values in $\mathbb N$.
In \cref{prop:qsound} a projector is the
orthogonal projection \texttt{K.starProjection} onto a subspace $K$ of a finite-dimensional complex
inner product space, and $P\le Q$ is inclusion of subspaces; \texttt{starProjection\_comp\_eq\_iff}
proves that this agrees with $QP=P$, and \texttt{starProjection\_orthogonal\_eq\_sub} that
$I-P$ projects onto $K^\perp$. In \cref{prop:classical,prop:qhume} operators are complex matrices,
a projector is a Hermitian idempotent matrix, a density operator is a positive semidefinite matrix of
trace $1$, and the Born probability is $\operatorname{Re}\Tr(\rho P)$. In \cref{prop:classical}
the order $P\le Q$ is used in its algebraic form $QP=P$, and in \cref{prop:qhume} $\ket\up$ and
$\ket\down$ are the standard basis vectors of $\mathbb C^2$. In part~(2) of \cref{prop:qhume},
``$\ket\up$ is the maximum-likelihood state'' is proved in the form: the likelihood of $\ket\up$
is at least that of every density operator. \Cref{prop:unitary,prop:noise} use the same matrix model, with
unitarity written $U^\dagger U=I$; \cref{prop:nondistrib} uses the lattice of subspaces of
$\mathbb C^2$; and the entropy $S(\rho)$ is $\sum_k-\lambda_k\ln\lambda_k$ over the roots of the
characteristic polynomial, which for Hermitian $\rho$ are its eigenvalues
(\texttt{vonNeumannEntropy\_eq\_sum\_eigenvalues}).
