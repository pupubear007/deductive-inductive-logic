\section{Lean formalization}\label{app:formalization}

All results in Sections~\ref{sec:deduction}--\ref{sec:foundation} are formalized in Lean~4
(toolchain \texttt{v4.34.1}) in the file \path{lean/WangLogic/Basic.lean} accompanying this
paper, using only the core library. \Cref{prop:qsound,prop:classical,prop:qhume} are formalized in
\path{lean/WangLogic/Quantum.lean} with Mathlib~\cite{mathlib} (commit
\texttt{d13f23b7}). Neither file contains \texttt{sorry}, and every declaration depends at most
on the axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound} and \texttt{Classical.choice}. The project
builds with
\begin{center}\texttt{cd lean \&\& lake exe cache get \&\& lake build}\end{center}
The file \path{lean/ComparatorChallenges/InductionNotDeduction.json} states
\cref{thm:hume} for independent checking with the Comparator tool. \Cref{rem:probability} and the
informal claims of \cref{sec:quantum} (unitary evolution, the failure of distributivity, entropy,
implementations) are not formalized.

\paragraph{Conventions for \cref{sec:quantum}.} In \cref{prop:qsound} a projector is the
orthogonal projection \texttt{K.starProjection} onto a subspace $K$ of a finite-dimensional complex
inner product space, and $P\le Q$ is inclusion of subspaces; \texttt{starProjection\_comp\_eq\_iff}
proves that this agrees with $QP=P$, and \texttt{starProjection\_orthogonal\_eq\_sub} that
$I-P$ projects onto $K^\perp$. In \cref{prop:classical,prop:qhume} operators are complex matrices,
a projector is a Hermitian idempotent matrix, a density operator is a positive semidefinite matrix of
trace $1$, and the Born probability is $\operatorname{Re}\Tr(\rho P)$. In \cref{prop:classical}
the order $P\le Q$ is used in its algebraic form $QP=P$, and in \cref{prop:qhume} $\ket\up$ and
$\ket\down$ are the standard basis vectors of $\mathbb C^2$. In part~(2) of \cref{prop:qhume},
``$\ket\up$ is the maximum-likelihood state'' is proved in the form: the likelihood of $\ket\up$
is at least that of every density operator.
