\section{The diagrammatic framework}\label{sec:framework}

The diagram has two primary rows and a foundation (\cref{fig:framework}).

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Deduction}, $\varphi\to\exists$. A thought $\varphi$ (premises) leads to an
        existence $\exists$ (a conclusion) along a straight arrow, read \emph{hence}. The passage
        is certain. Polarity $\up$ marks an affirmed conclusion and $\down$ a negated one.
  \item \textbf{Induction}, $s\exists\circlearrowright t\varphi$. The study of existence $s\exists$
        (observations) leads to a theory of thought $t\varphi$ (a hypothesis) along a circular
        arrow, read \emph{conclude}. The passage is tentative and is repeated as new observations
        arrive.
  \item \textbf{Foundation}. Iterations $i$, hypotheses $h$ and levels $l$, together with binary
        states $0,1$ and polarities $\up,\down$, generate the thoughts used above.
\end{itemize}

\begin{figure}[ht]
\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}[
    node distance=12mm and 30mm,
    box/.style={draw,rounded corners,minimum width=28mm,minimum height=9mm,align=center},
    found/.style={draw,dotted,circle,minimum size=10mm,inner sep=1pt},
    >={Stealth[length=2.5mm]}]
  \node[box] (phi) {thought $\varphi$};
  \node[box,right=of phi] (ex) {existence $\exists^{\up/\down}$};
  \draw[->,thick] (phi) -- node[above]{\small hence} node[below]{\small certain} (ex);

  \node[box,below=of phi] (s) {study $s\exists$};
  \node[box,below=of ex] (t) {theory $t\varphi^{\up/\down}$};
  \draw[->,thick] (s) to[bend left=18] node[above]{\small conclude} (t);
  \draw[->,thick,dashed] (t) to[bend left=18] node[below]{\small iterate $i$: new observations} (s);

  \draw[->,densely dotted] (ex) -- node[right,align=left]{\small observed}(t.north -| ex.south);
  \coordinate (mid) at ($(s.south)!0.5!(t.south)$);
  \node[found,below=14mm of mid,xshift=-36mm] (i) {$i$};
  \node[found,right=8mm of i] (h) {$h$};
  \node[found,right=8mm of h] (l) {$l$};
  \node[found,right=8mm of l] (b) {$0,1$};
  \node[found,right=8mm of b] (p) {$\up,\down$};
  \draw[red!70!black,dashed] (i) -- (h) -- (l) -- (b) -- (p);
  \node[below=3mm of l] {\small foundation $m : I\times H\times L\times\{0,1\}\to\Phi$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\caption{Deduction (top row), induction (middle row) and the foundation (bottom).}
\label{fig:framework}
\end{figure}

\Cref{tab:symbols} fixes the reading of each symbol. The symbol $\exists$ is kept for the
diagram only; the set of possible existences is written $\W$, so that it is not confused with the
existential quantifier.
